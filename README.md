# EZDRM

### Digital Rights Management. Simplified.

EZDRM provides cloud-based **Digital Rights Management as a Service
(DRMaaS)** for content owners and streaming media businesses. Founded in 2003,
EZDRM focuses on helping customers protect and monetize video without having
to build and operate their own DRM infrastructure.

Our approach is to make content protection easier to integrate, manage, and
scale, while using established technologies and working alongside the
streaming platforms and services our customers already use.

## What We Do

- **Managed DRM services:** Cloud-hosted DRM technology and license delivery,
  managed by a team focused on content protection.
- **Multi-DRM support:** Solutions for Google Widevine, Apple FairPlay,
  Microsoft PlayReady, and Huawei WisePlay, helping services reach devices
  with different DRM requirements.
- **Integration resources:** Documentation and partner guidance to help
  connect DRM with encoding, packaging, and playback workflows.
- **Flexible service options:** Configurable license-delivery business rules
  and service options designed to accommodate different streaming needs.
